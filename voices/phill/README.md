# Your voice sample (for cloning)

The voice clone copies **how you sound in this one recording**: your tone, your accent, and your energy. Record it in the exact style you want your videos to sound like.

## What to record
- **15–20 seconds.** The model mostly listens to the **first 10 seconds**, so **start talking right away**, with no silence and no "uh, okay".
- **Your hyped YouTube voice.** If you read it flat, every video comes out flat.
- **One clean take.** No music, no echo, no fan or TV in the background.

Read this (or anything in your own words that sounds like you):

> Doctor Doom doesn't just beat the Avengers. He kills the gods! And honestly? That's not even the craziest part. Because once you see what Marvel's been planting for years, you can't unsee it. So let's break it down, because this is about to get wild.

## How to record
- **Phone:** use the voice memo app. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, a little off to the side so your breath doesn't pop. A small room with a bed, couch or clothes around works best. Bathrooms and kitchens echo.
- **Computer mic:** any recording app works (Audacity is free).
- Any format is fine: `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`.

## Where to put it
Save it in this folder as **`sample.m4a`** (or `.mp3` / `.wav`).

Want a different mood later (serious, sad, angry)? Record extra samples named `sample_serious.m4a` and so on. A section of the script can use them (see `profile.yaml`).

Your recording is never changed. The tool makes a cleaned-up copy in this folder called `reference.wav`.
