## Comics
- **CONFIRMED (loosely).** Kevin Feige points to *Secret Wars* as the inspiration, "kinda." [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/kevin-feige-confirms-marvel-comic-inspired-avengers-doomsday/)
- Background: Jonathan Hickman's *Avengers* / *New Avengers* run, **"Time Runs Out"**, leads into ***Secret Wars* (2015)**. **Incursions** are universes colliding and destroying each other. Doom ends up as **God Emperor Doom**, ruling Battleworld, a patchwork planet built from dead universes. [Looper](https://looper.com/1491328/what-avengers-secret-wars-about-marvel-comics), [Nerdist](https://nerdist.com/article/marvel-incursions-one-shots-revisits-avengers-doomsday-inspiration/)
- **CONFIRMED.** Marvel is publishing five *Incursions* one-shots as a tie-in. *X-Men: Incursions #1* is out Nov 18, 2026. [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/687419-marvel-announces-secret-wars-incursion-project-avengers-doomsday-approaches), [Bleeding Cool](https://bleedingcool.com/comics/marvel-launches-new-incursions-one-shots-ahead-of-avengers-doomsday/)
- **THEORY.** The biggest open question is why Doom has Tony Stark's face. Is he a Tony variant or something else? Marvel hasn't explained it. A Comicbook.com piece says one Doom theory is "all but confirmed" and another is debunked. Read it before using it: [Comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/movies/feature/marvel-all-but-confirms-1-doctor-doom-theory-for-doomsday-may-have-debunked-another/)
