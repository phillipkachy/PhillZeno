## SEGMENT 1: Incursions

Okay. Incursions. This is the engine of the whole story, so let's get it right.

[IMAGE: two Earths overlapping in the sky, comic panel]

In Hickman's comics, the multiverse starts dying. Two universes start crashing into each other.

[IMAGE: two universes colliding, cosmic comic art]

And the point where they collide? It's always Earth.

[IMAGE: Incursion panel, a second Earth filling the sky]

Picture it. You look up, and there's another Earth in the sky. Getting closer. And it's not a vision. It's real.

[IMAGE: Black Panther looking up at a red sky, New Avengers #1]

The first person to see one is Black Panther. In Wakanda. He watches another Earth fill the sky, and he watches heroes from that world show up trying to save it.

[IMAGE: Black Panther in Wakanda, incursion overhead]
[IMAGE: alternate-Earth heroes arriving, New Avengers #1]

When the two Earths touch, both universes die. Everything. Every planet, every person, gone.

[IMAGE: universe being wiped out, white light panel]

And here's the brutal part. There's only one way to stop it. You destroy the other Earth before they touch.

[IMAGE: a second Earth exploding in space]

Your world lives. Theirs doesn't. Billions of people, gone, so yours can keep going.

[IMAGE: debris of a destroyed Earth]

Now if that sounds familiar, it should. Marvel already planted this. In Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, we literally heard the word.

[CLIP: Multiverse of Madness, incursion explanation scene]

That movie even gave us an Illuminati. And who was sitting in that room? Patrick Stewart's Professor X.

[IMAGE: the Earth-838 Illuminati, Multiverse of Madness]
[IMAGE: Patrick Stewart as Professor X in Multiverse of Madness]

Then Strange caused an incursion himself. Clea showed up in the post-credit scene and told him so.

[IMAGE: Clea and Strange, Multiverse of Madness post-credits]

That wasn't a throwaway line. That was a setup.

[IMAGE: Doctor Strange with his third eye]

And this is where it gets dark. Because in the comics, somebody has to decide which Earth dies.

[IMAGE: an Earth in the crosshairs, comic panel]

And the people making that call? They're heroes.

[IMAGE: the Illuminati gathered in shadow]

## SEGMENT 2: The Illuminati's Choice

Meet the Illuminati. Marvel's secret club of geniuses and kings.

[IMAGE: Illuminati group shot, Hickman era]

Reed Richards. Tony Stark. Doctor Strange. Black Panther. Black Bolt. Namor. And Beast. The smartest people on the planet, meeting in secret.

[IMAGE: Reed Richards and Tony Stark at a table]
[IMAGE: Black Panther and Namor facing off]

And when the Incursions start, they're the ones who have to deal with it. No public. No Avengers. Just them.

[IMAGE: Illuminati debating, dark room]

Now Captain America was in the room early on. And their first plan? The Infinity Gauntlet.

[IMAGE: the Infinity Gauntlet, New Avengers]

Cap puts it on. He pushes the other Earth back. And the Gems shatter. Just like that, their best weapon's gone.

[IMAGE: Captain America wearing the Gauntlet]
[IMAGE: Infinity Gems shattering]

So the geniuses start talking about the other option. Destroying worlds. And Cap says what you'd expect Cap to say. No. We find another way.

[IMAGE: Captain America arguing with the Illuminati]

So what do they do? They wipe his memory. Doctor Strange goes into Steve Rogers' head and erases the whole thing.

[IMAGE: Doctor Strange casting a spell on Captain America]

Then they send him home like nothing happened.

[IMAGE: Captain America walking away, unaware]

Let that sink in. The good guys mind-wiped Captain America. Because they knew he'd stop them.

[IMAGE: Captain America alone, confused]

And even then, when the moment comes, most of them can't pull the trigger. Reed can't. Tony can't.

[IMAGE: Reed Richards hesitating]

It takes Namor. He does it, and he wipes out a world.

[IMAGE: Namor standing over a destroyed Earth]

Then Namor goes further. He teams up with the worst villains around. Thanos. The Black Order.

[IMAGE: Namor with Thanos]

They call themselves the Cabal. And they destroy Earths and actually enjoy it.

[IMAGE: the Cabal, Namor with Thanos and the Black Order]

So now you've got heroes doing monstrous things, and villains doing it for fun.

[IMAGE: Illuminati and Cabal, split image]

But here's the twist. Nobody's asking who's actually causing the Incursions. And the answer is way bigger than anybody thought.

[IMAGE: cracked, broken multiverse imagery]

## SEGMENT 3: Time Runs Out

This is where Hickman flips the whole thing upside down. Time Runs Out picks up eight months later. And the Avengers are at war with each other.

[IMAGE: Time Runs Out cover, heroes fighting]

Cap got his memories back. And he's furious. He's hunting the Illuminati, and especially Tony.

[IMAGE: Captain America versus Iron Man, Time Runs Out]

Meanwhile, the multiverse keeps collapsing. And finally, they find out why. It's the Beyonders.

[IMAGE: the Beyonders, cosmic beings in white]

These are beings so powerful they're above the cosmic gods. They're killing the multiverse on purpose.

[IMAGE: Beyonders looming over the multiverse]

They even take out the Living Tribunal. That's how big we're talking.

[IMAGE: Living Tribunal destroyed, comic panel]

So who steps up? Not Cap. Not Reed. Doctor Doom.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom, classic comic pose]

Doom teams up with Molecule Man, a guy with reality-bending power. And Doom uses that power to do the unthinkable. He kills the Beyonders.

[IMAGE: Doom and Molecule Man together]
[IMAGE: Doom confronting the Beyonders]

Read that again. Doctor Doom kills the beings who were killing God. And he takes their power for himself.

[IMAGE: Doom glowing with cosmic power]

But it's still too late. The last two Earths collide. The main Marvel universe and the Ultimate universe. Everything ends.

[IMAGE: the final incursion, two Earths colliding, Secret Wars #1]

Everything, except what Doom saves. And what he builds next is where Doomsday is clearly heading.

[IMAGE: white void, the end of everything]

## SEGMENT 4: God Emperor Doom

This is Secret Wars. Nine issues, 2015. And it's Doom's world now.

[IMAGE: Secret Wars #1 cover]

Out of the scraps of dead universes, Doom builds one planet. Battleworld. A patchwork of realities stitched together. Different eras, different heroes, all on one map.

[IMAGE: Battleworld map]
[IMAGE: Battleworld landscape, mixed realities]

There's a zone full of Marvel Zombies, walled off. There's a wasteland where Old Man Logan lives. Every corner is some reality that used to be its own universe.

[IMAGE: the Deadlands, Marvel Zombies on Battleworld]
[IMAGE: Old Man Logan, Secret Wars tie-in]

And at the center? His capital city. Doomstadt. Remember that throne room in the trailer? A lot of fans think that's exactly where we are.

[IMAGE: Doomstadt castle, comic]
[IMAGE: the throne shot from the Doomsday trailer]

Doom rules it all as God Emperor. He's got Doctor Strange as his right hand, keeping order. He's got an army of Thors as his police.

[IMAGE: Doctor Strange as sheriff of Battleworld]
[IMAGE: the Thor Corps]

And here's the part that's genuinely chilling. Doom has Sue Storm as his wife.

[IMAGE: Doom with Sue Storm]

He's raising Franklin and Valeria as his own kids. He took Reed's family.

[IMAGE: Doom with Franklin and Valeria]

And nobody remembers the old world. To them, this is just how it's always been. They're living lives Doom handed them.

[IMAGE: people of Battleworld, everyday life]

Now think about that trailer line. "All of you have lived stolen lives." Doom pulling Sentinels out of the ground, from a universe that isn't his. That looks a lot like a guy collecting pieces of dead worlds.

[IMAGE: Doom with the Sentinels, D23 trailer]
[IMAGE: Sentinel close-up from the trailer]

And look at who's in this movie. MCU heroes. Fox's X-Men. A Fantastic Four from their own universe. That's a lot of different worlds ending up in one place.

[IMAGE: Fox X-Men cast, Doomsday promo]
[IMAGE: Fantastic Four cast, Doomsday promo]

That's a theory, to be clear. Marvel hasn't confirmed any of it. But the pieces line up way too well.

[IMAGE: side by side, Battleworld comic and Doomsday trailer frame]

So if Doom is a god, how do you beat a god?

[IMAGE: Doom on his throne, looking down]

In the comics, the answer isn't a punch. It's a question.

[IMAGE: Reed Richards facing Doom, Secret Wars #9]

## PAYOFF

The final battle of Secret Wars is Reed Richards versus Victor von Doom. Molecule Man cuts Doom off from his power, and suddenly it's just two men.

[IMAGE: Reed and Doom fighting, Secret Wars #9]
[IMAGE: Doom, unmasked and scarred]

And Reed pushes him to admit the thing he's never admitted. That if Reed had the power, he would've done better.

[IMAGE: Doom's face, defeated]

And Doom admits it. That's the whole story.

[IMAGE: Doom's face, defeated, close-up]

Doom's not a monster who wants to destroy everything. He's a genius who saved what he could, and couldn't stand that Reed might be better.

[IMAGE: Doom kneeling]

Then Reed gets the power, and he rebuilds the multiverse. Fresh start. And he does one more thing. He heals Doom's face.

[IMAGE: Reed rebuilding the multiverse, Secret Wars #9]
[IMAGE: Doom's healed face, end of Secret Wars]

The man who stole his family. Reed fixes him anyway. That's Reed Richards.

[IMAGE: Reed and Sue reunited]

Now look at the MCU. Pedro Pascal's Reed is already in the Doomsday trailer, standing in a ruined city, facing Doom. Doomsday then Secret Wars. Two movies. Same directors as Infinity War and Endgame.

[IMAGE: Pedro Pascal as Reed facing Doom, D23 trailer]
[IMAGE: Russo brothers]

If Marvel's following this story even halfway, Doomsday is Time Runs Out. The collapse. And Secret Wars is Battleworld, and the rebuild.

[IMAGE: Doomsday and Secret Wars logos together]

So here's what I'm watching for on December eighteenth. A second Earth in the sky. Doom with way more power than he should have.

[IMAGE: incursion sky, comic panel]

And Reed and Doom, face to face, with everything on the line.

[IMAGE: Reed and Doom face to face, comic]

And Doom wearing Tony Stark's face? In a story about a genius who couldn't stand being second best?

[IMAGE: Tony Stark, Endgame]

That's not a coincidence. That's the point.

[IMAGE: RDJ as Doom, mask half on]
