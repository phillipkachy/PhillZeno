## NUMBER 7: The Power Cosmic

Alright, number seven. Doom steals the Power Cosmic.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom with the Power Cosmic, Fantastic Four #57]

Back in 1966, Doom tricked the Silver Surfer and stole the Power Cosmic. The same energy Galactus gives his heralds.

[IMAGE: Silver Surfer, classic Kirby art]
[IMAGE: Galactus, classic Kirby art]

And think about that for a second. This is a guy in armor with no powers of his own, who looks at the herald of a planet eater and says, I'll take that.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom in armor, classic Kirby panel]
[IMAGE: Doom and the Silver Surfer face to face]

He uses it to go after the Fantastic Four, and he almost wins. The only reason he loses is that he flies into the barrier Galactus left around Earth, and it knocks the power right off him.

[IMAGE: Fantastic Four vs Doctor Doom, Fantastic Four #60]
[IMAGE: Fantastic Four classic team shot]

So Doom didn't lose to the Fantastic Four. He lost on a technicality.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom defeated, classic comic]

And that's number seven. It's the lowest one on this list.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom classic portrait]

## NUMBER 6: Doomwar

Alright, number six. Doomwar.

[IMAGE: Doomwar #1 cover]

Doom invades Wakanda, the most advanced nation on Earth, and goes straight for its vibranium vault.

[IMAGE: Wakanda, comic panel]
[IMAGE: Doom's army invading Wakanda, Doomwar]

He can't crack the code, so he captures Storm and makes T'Challa choose. His wife, or his country's vibranium.

[IMAGE: Storm captured by Doctor Doom, Doomwar]
[IMAGE: Black Panther T'Challa, Doomwar]

T'Challa of course chooses Storm, and Doom walks out with the vibranium.

[IMAGE: Black Panther and Storm, Doomwar]

Then Doom uses science and magic to link himself to every piece of vibranium on the planet.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom with vibranium, Doomwar]

The only way T'Challa stops him is by destroying all of Wakanda's vibranium himself.

[IMAGE: Black Panther Doomwar cover]

And now, just think about how scary that is. To beat Doom, Black Panther had to sacrifice his entire nation's greatest resource.

[IMAGE: Doomwar cover, Doom over Wakanda]

That's not even a win. I feel like that's more like damage control.

[IMAGE: Black Panther brooding]

## NUMBER 5: Emperor Doom

Number five. Emperor Doom.

[IMAGE: Emperor Doom graphic novel cover 1987]

In 1987, in a graphic novel, Doom uses the Purple Man, a villain whose power is mind control, to power a machine that takes over the mind of every person on Earth. And it actually works.

[IMAGE: Purple Man, Marvel comics]
[IMAGE: Doctor Doom mind control machine, Emperor Doom]

Doom ends up conquering the entire planet, Avengers included.

[IMAGE: Emperor Doom ruling the world, Avengers]

And here's my favorite detail. The Purple Man tries his power on Doom himself, face to face, and it does not work.

[IMAGE: Purple Man close-up]

Doom's will is just too strong. So the guy whose whole thing is controlling minds can't control Doctor Doom's mind.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom face, intense]

So Doom controls the entire planet, and he doesn't even crack the top three.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom over Earth]

## NUMBER 4: Triumph and Torment

Number four. Triumph and Torment.

[IMAGE: Doctor Strange and Doctor Doom Triumph and Torment cover]

If you have never read Doctor Strange and Doctor Doom: Triumph and Torment, do yourself a favor and read it.

[IMAGE: Triumph and Torment graphic novel art]

The best sorcerers on Earth compete to become the Sorcerer Supreme. Strange ends up winning it, but Doom comes in second.

[IMAGE: Doctor Strange casting a spell, classic]
[IMAGE: Doctor Doom using magic]

Second place out of every magic user on the planet, and he's a scientist.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom in his lab]

And Doom didn't compete for a title. He did it to save his mother's soul, which had been trapped by Mephisto since he was a kid.

[IMAGE: Cynthia von Doom]
[IMAGE: Mephisto, Marvel comics]

So Doom and Strange go into hell itself and face the devil. Doom stands up to Mephisto on Mephisto's own turf, and they beat him at his own game.

[IMAGE: Mephisto's realm, hell, comic panel]
[IMAGE: Doctor Doom vs Mephisto]

This is the moment that shows you who Doom really is. Everything he does, even the evil stuff, comes from somewhere real.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom unmasked, emotional]

Alright, number three. Doom becomes Sorcerer Supreme.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom as Sorcerer Supreme]

And this one is recent. During the Blood Hunt event, Doctor Strange gets turned into a vampire and asks Doom for help.

[IMAGE: Blood Hunt cover]
[IMAGE: Doctor Strange vampire, Blood Hunt]

Doom agrees, but he needs the title of Sorcerer Supreme to do it.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom with the Eye of Agamotto]

He does promise to give it back afterwards. And of course, that's the trick. He never does.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom Sorcerer Supreme, Blood Hunt]

And then, in One World Under Doom, he takes over every broadcast on Earth and declares himself Emperor of the World.

[IMAGE: One World Under Doom #1 cover]
[IMAGE: One World Under Doom, Doom addresses the world]

And the crazy part is that a lot of people actually welcome it.

[IMAGE: One World Under Doom crowd]

The heroes are trying to stop him, and some of the world is like, honestly, things are actually working better with Doom in charge.

## NUMBER 2: Secret Wars 1984

Number two. In the original Secret Wars, a godlike being called the Beyonder pulls heroes and villains to a planet to fight.

[IMAGE: Secret Wars 1984 #1 cover]
[IMAGE: The Beyonder, Secret Wars 1984]

Everyone else is fighting each other while Doom is studying the Beyonder.

[IMAGE: Secret Wars 1984 heroes battle]

And then he steals the Beyonder's power. Like, all of it.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom steals the Beyonder's power, Secret Wars 1984]

For a moment, Doctor Doom is basically a god. He can do anything.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom godlike, Secret Wars 1984]

And what ends up undoing him isn't a hero. It's Doom himself. He can't stop doubting, and the power slips away.

[IMAGE: Secret Wars 1984 #10 cover]
[IMAGE: Doctor Doom doubting, Secret Wars]

That's what makes Doctor Doom so interesting. The only person who can really beat Doom is Doom himself.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom brooding, Latveria]

## NUMBER 1: Secret Wars 2015

And finally, number one.

[IMAGE: Secret Wars 2015 #1 cover]

Secret Wars 2015. The entire multiverse is collapsing. Everything is ending.

[IMAGE: Incursion, two Earths colliding]

And Doom, with help from the Molecule Man and Doctor Strange, faces the beings responsible, the Beyonders.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom and the Molecule Man]
[IMAGE: The Beyonders, Hickman]

He beats them, and takes their power.

[IMAGE: Doctor Doom confronts the Beyonders]

Then he takes what's left of reality and builds a whole new world out of it.

[IMAGE: Battleworld map]

Battleworld. And he rules it as God Emperor Doom for years.

[IMAGE: God Emperor Doom on his throne]

He decides who lives, what history is, even who people remember.

[IMAGE: Battleworld domains]

He doesn't just beat the universe. He rewrites it.

[IMAGE: Secret Wars 2015 Doom cover]

And in the end, when it's just him and Reed Richards, Doom admits something huge. That Reed would have done it better.

[IMAGE: Reed Richards vs Doctor Doom, Secret Wars #9]

After all that power, the thing that matters most to Doom is beating Reed.

[IMAGE: Reed Richards and Doctor Doom face to face]

That's why this is number one. His greatest power and his biggest weakness in the same moment.
