## Who I am talking to

One person: a DC fan in their 20s who grew up on the DC animated movies, Young Justice and the
Batfamily comics, follows every DCU announcement from James Gunn, and has opinions about which
Robin matters most. Watches Kaythegod talks and Comic Lads. (inferred)

## How I actually talk

- A fan talking to a friend, not a critic giving a lecture. First person, opinion-forward,
  honest about bias: "I'll be honest, at first I was a little bit skeptical", "Nightwing is always
  going to be my favorite Robin."
- Admits a change of mind and walks the viewer through it: skeptical -> heard good arguments ->
  came around. That arc is a strength. Use it.
- Numbered structure: "I wrote down eight reasons", "Point number two", "Reason number five",
  "Number seven". Viewers always know where they are.
- Opens on the news context (James Gunn, the DCU, an announcement), then states the take.
- Big, warm enthusiasm for what he loves: "do yourself a favor, shut down this video and go watch
  those movies", "I love those movies with a passion", "chef's kiss", "absolutely peak",
  "I cannot wait to see a live action of that."
- Says what he's tired of, bluntly: "that grumpy older Batman trope which I'm so tired of",
  "That angle is lazy and completely misses the point."
- Credits other creators by name when he borrows an idea (credited Kaythegod talks on screen).
- Ends: "If you watched all the way through, I really appreciate it. Give it a like, subscribe to
  help me keep making these videos, and I'll see you in the next one."

## Words I never use

(inferred — Phill to confirm) Corporate or essay words that do not sound spoken: "delve",
"tapestry", "furthermore", "in conclusion", "unlock", "game-changer", "let's dive deep".
No "Hey guys, welcome back to the channel" intro.

## Words I do use

"I'll be honest", "you know", "you know what I mean?", "if that makes sense", "chef's kiss",
"peak", "do yourself a favor", "with a passion", "I cannot wait", "thank God", "the DCU",
"James Gunn", "live action", "point number ___", "reason number ___".

In scripts: keep "you know" and "if that makes sense" to a few per video. On the recording they
read as natural; written into every paragraph they read as filler.

## What I will not claim

- Rumors or scoops stated as fact. Speculation is framed as "I think", "my guess", "if I were
  James Gunn". (inferred)
- Box office numbers, release dates, casting news or quotes without a source I can name.
- Ideas from other creators as my own — credit them.

## My format

- Voiceover over stills, comic panels and short movie/trailer moments. Not on camera.
- Target 12–16 minutes for the main videos (past videos ran 4–11; the channels he models run
  12–20).
- No channel intro. Open on the take within the first 15 seconds.
- Numbered list / "X reasons" structure is the default for opinion videos.
- Clean language by default; an occasional mild swear is fine. (inferred)
- Lane: DCU and MCU speculation (Kaythegod talks style) plus evergreen debates and rankings
  (Comic Lads style). Batfamily, Nightwing and Damian Wayne are home turf.
